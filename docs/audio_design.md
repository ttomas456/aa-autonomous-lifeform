# Audio Design

All sounds are generated in `scripts/procedural_sound.gd`, so the project does not rely on third-party audio assets.

## Sound Events

- Player whistle: played when pressing `Q`.
- Dog bark: delayed response after whistle and some follow states.
- Happy yip: petting and play feedback.
- Whine: seeking food.
- Chew: eating food.
- Toy squeak: toy play.
- Snore: sleeping.
- Click: item placement.
- Background music: quiet looping procedural pad.

## Mix Notes

The dog and world sounds are intentionally quiet so they do not overpower the demo. The background track is softer again and is meant to make the garden feel less empty while leaving creature sounds readable.
