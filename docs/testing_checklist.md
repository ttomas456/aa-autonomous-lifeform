# Testing Checklist

Run this before the final export and video.

- Project opens in Godot 4.5.
- Main scene launches without script errors.
- Buster and Cleo spawn in the yard.
- Dogs wander without player input.
- Player movement works with `WASD` and arrow keys.
- Left click spawns food and food expires.
- Right click spawns toys and toys expire.
- Hungry dogs seek and eat food.
- Dogs react to toys with play behaviour.
- `E` pets the nearest dog and increases trust.
- `Q` plays the whistle and dogs respond.
- `G` toggles debug gizmos.
- Background music plays quietly.
- Barking, yips, chewing, toy squeak, snore, and click sounds are audible but not too loud.
