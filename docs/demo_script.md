# Demo Script

Use this order when recording the final video from an exported build.

1. Show Buster and Cleo wandering without input.
2. Explain that each dog has hunger, energy, happiness, trust, and personality values.
3. Move the player near the dogs and show that they follow.
4. Press `E` near a dog to pet it and show the trust/emotion reaction.
5. Press `Q` to whistle and show the whistle sound plus delayed barking response.
6. Drop food with left click and let a hungry dog eat.
7. Drop a toy with right click and show play behaviour.
8. Toggle `G` to show debug gizmos and explain the current target lines.
9. Finish by summarising the FSM, steering, memory, procedural animation, particles, and sound.
